# Financial Research Memorandum: Kweichow Moutai Co., Ltd. （600519）

> Reporting period: 2025/1/1–6/30 | Disclosure date: 2025-08-13 | Analysis date: 2026-10-15
> reporting basis: CNY yuan, consolidated statements as primary, Accounting Standards for Business Enterprises, semi-annual report unaudited [Current filing PDF p. 2]
> One-sentence judgment: Revenue and net profit attributable to owners of the parent still grew by approximately 9%, but operating cash flow year over year decreased by 64.18%, mainly due to deposits and reserves at the finance company on a reporting basis basis, rather than deterioration in collection from the core business; Contract liabilities decreased by 42.59% from the beginning of the year, which is the most important demand-side signal to track this period.
> Scope of evidence: this period's 2025 pages of the semi-annual report for the year 1—8、11、14、15、17、21、24—34、39、43—45、49—51、78 page; In the prior period 2024 pages of the semi-annual report for the year attached to the 2、5—7、15、16、26—28、30、34、42、46、57、81、86 page. Only the attached pages are covered; the complete notes and segment details were not obtained.

## I. Research Conclusions and Key Changes

**Change One: Revenue growth slowed to single digits, but the structure did not deteriorate.** Operating revenue for the current period was 893.89 hundred million, compared with 819.31 hundred million in the same period last year, an increase of 9.10%; Total operating revenue was 910.94 hundred million, an increase of 9.16% [Current filing PDF p. 5] [Current filing PDF p. 28]. In the prior period, on the same reporting basis, operating revenue growth was 17.76% [Prior filing PDF p. 5]. The original text for the reason for the current period change only states "mainly due to increased sales volume this period" [Current filing PDF p. 7], without mentioning price adjustments, which differs from the prior period's statement of "increased sales volume and sales price adjustments for major products" [Prior filing PDF p. 7]. Business implication: The company's description of growth drivers changed from mentioning both sales volume and price adjustments in the prior period to mentioning only sales volume in the current period; There is a lack of direct evidence for the price contribution in the current period, and it cannot be concluded on this basis that price made no contribution. Judgment condition: If sales volume growth continues to decline in the second half of the year while price makes no positive contribution, the center of revenue growth will shift further downward.

**Change Two: Operating cash flow fell sharply, but the main reason lies in the financial subsidiary on a reporting basis.** net cash from operating activities was 131.19 hundred million, compared with 366.22 hundred million in the same period last year, a decrease of 64.18% [Current filing PDF p. 5]. The original text attributes this to "a decrease in deposits absorbed from member units of the group company by Guizhou Moutai Group Finance Co., Ltd., a controlled subsidiary, and an increase in statutory reserves deposited with the central bank and non-withdrawable interbank deposits" [Current filing PDF p. 5]. The main statement corroborates this: the net increase in customer deposits and interbank deposits changed from +114.90 hundred million last year to -25.17 hundred million this period, and the net increase in deposits with the central bank and interbank deposits rose from 71.97 hundred million to 254.11 hundred million [Current filing PDF p. 32]. Cash received from the sale of goods and provision of services in the same period was 950.87 hundred million, compared with 870.94 hundred million in the previous year, an increase of 9.18%[Current filing PDF p. 31], which is basically in line with the revenue growth rate. Business implication: The decline in cash flow mainly came from the liability side of the finance company and reserve occupation, while collections from the liquor core business did not deteriorate in tandem. Counter-evidence condition: If the growth rate of cash received from sales of goods continues to be lower than revenue growth, channel collections need to be reassessed.

**Change Three: Contract liabilities fell sharply from the beginning of the year.** Contract liabilities were 55.07 hundred million, compared with 95.92 hundred million at the end of last year, a decrease of 42.59%, and the original text attributes this to "a decrease in advance payments from distributors" [Current filing PDF p. 8]. In the same period, notes receivable rose from 19.84 hundred million to 28.16 hundred million, an increase of 41.90%, and the original text attributes this to "an increase in sales business accepted by Jiangxiang Liquor Marketing Co., Ltd. through bank acceptance bills" [Current filing PDF p. 8]. Business implication: The decline in advance receipts and the rise in bill settlement occurred simultaneously, which may reflect a change in channel willingness to pay or settlement methods. Note: Changes from the beginning to the end of the period cannot be directly compared with revenue on a same-period year over year basis to infer improvement or deterioration in collections. Judgment condition: It is necessary to combine changes in contract liabilities and the number of distributors in the third quarter to determine whether this is seasonal or trend-based.

## II. Business Drivers and Profit Quality

**Revenue and gross profit.** Operating revenue was 893.89 hundred million （+9.10%）, operating costs were 77.77 hundred million （+15.21%）, cost growth was faster than revenue, and the gross margin fell from 91.76% to 91.30% (derived as operating revenue minus operating costs divided by operating revenue). The original text explains the change in costs as "increased sales volume and increased production costs" [Current filing PDF p. 7], without breaking down unit costs and mix. Taxes and surcharges were 139.42 hundred million, an increase of 3.33% [Current filing PDF p. 28], slower than revenue, related to the ad valorem and specific-quantity structure of consumption tax, but the itemized details for the current period are not obtained.

**Expense side.** Selling expenses were 32.60 hundred million, an increase of 24.56%, and the original text attributes this to "increased advertising and market expenses" [Current filing PDF p. 7]; Administrative expenses were 36.95 hundred million, a decrease of 3.68%; R&D expenses were 0.74 hundred million, a decrease of 20.03%; Financial expenses were -4.87 hundred million, compared with -8.95 hundred million in the same period last year, and the original text attributes this to "a decline in commercial bank deposit interest rates" [Current filing PDF p. 7]. The growth rate of selling expenses was significantly higher than revenue, which is one of the main explainable factors for why the current period's profit growth rate （8.89%） was slightly lower than the revenue growth rate （9.10%）.

**Profit and attribution.** Total profit was 627.78 hundred million （+9.03%）, net profit was 469.87 hundred million, of which net profit attributable to owners of the parent was 454.03 hundred million （+8.89%）, and non-controlling interests were 15.84 hundred million (14.81 hundred million last year) [Current filing PDF p. 29]. Attributable to parent + minority interests = 469.87 hundred million, consistent with consolidated net profit. net profit attributable to owners of the parent excluding non-recurring items was 453.90 hundred million （+8.93%）, total non-recurring gains and losses were only 0.13 hundred million [Current filing PDF pp. 5—6], and profit excluding non-recurring items differed very little from attributable to parent, so profit quality was not disturbed by one-off items.

**Alternative explanation.** Revenue growth may also partly come from changes in channel structure (direct sales share) rather than terminal sell-through. The current period sales table lists direct sales of 400.10 hundred million and wholesale agency of 493.43 hundred million [Current filing PDF p. 8], but comparable figures for the same table in the prior period are not obtained, so structural changes cannot be calculated, and therefore no inference is made about changes in the direct sales share.

## III. Asset Quality, Cash, and Solvency

**Linkage from profit to cash.** net profit attributable to owners of the parent was 454.03 hundred million, and operating cash flow was 131.19 hundred million; Attributable to parent profit and consolidated operating cash flow are not a complete reconciliation statement on the same reporting basis. In the original statement, the net increase in customer deposits and interbank deposits for the current period was -2,516,653,584.50 currency units, and the net increase in deposits with the central bank and interbank deposits for the current period was 25,410,788,169.25 currency units; the latter is listed on the cash outflow side, so the combined impact of the two items in the current period was -27,927,441,753.75 currency units (approximately 279.27 hundred million net occupation) [Current filing PDF p. 32]. Compared with the same period last year, the former decreased by 14,006,487,914.49 currency units, and the latter's cash outflow increased by 18,214,197,406.25 currency units, for a combined year over year negative impact of 32,220,685,320.74 currency units (approximately 322.21 hundred million); After offsetting other items, consolidated operating cash flow actually decreased by 23,502,772,781.30 currency units on a year over year basis (approximately 235.03 hundred million) [Current filing PDF p. 32]. The ratio of cash received from sales of goods of 950.87 hundred million to operating revenue of 893.89 hundred million is approximately 1.06 [Current filing PDF p. 31] [Current filing PDF p. 5]. This is a separate observation of sales cash receipts, not core business operating cash flow after excluding the impact of the finance company, and it cannot be used on this basis alone to conclude that collection quality is normal.

**Cash bridge.** Cash and cash equivalents at the beginning of the period were 1699.70 hundred million, plus operating 131.19 hundred million, investing -3.14 hundred million, financing -398.93 hundred million, and exchange rate +0.03 hundred million, giving 1428.85 hundred million at the end of the period [Current filing PDF p. 33], consistent with the ending balance in the notes [Current filing PDF p. 78]. Financing outflows were mainly dividend distribution of 346.71 hundred million and other cash paid relating to financing activities of 52.22 hundred million (the original text attributes this to "share repurchase" [Current filing PDF p. 7]).

**Restricted funds.** Statutory deposit reserves of 82.14 hundred million are listed as "monetary funds not belonging to cash and cash equivalents," with the reason "restricted use" [Current filing PDF p. 78]. Monetary funds of 516.45 hundred million include bank deposits of 516.45 hundred million [Current filing PDF p. 50], but the portion related to the finance company cannot be directly regarded as freely available cash for debt repayment. Lending funds of 1263.51 hundred million are mainly interbank deposits [Current filing PDF p. 51], which are assets of the finance company and should not be conflated with cash available to the liquor core business.

**Debt and capital expenditure.** The consolidated statements have no short-term borrowings, long-term borrowings, or bonds payable [Current filing PDF pp. 25—26]. Cash paid for the purchase and construction of fixed assets, intangible assets, and other long-term assets was 15.96 hundred million [Current filing PDF p. 32], and the simplified cash balance (operating cash flow minus this expenditure) was 115.23 hundred million. Construction in progress 27.62 hundred million, compared with the beginning of the year 21.50 hundred million increased by 28.49%[Current filing PDF p. 24], and capacity construction is still underway. Inventory was 549.72 hundred million, an increase of 1.16% from the beginning of the year [Current filing PDF p. 8], which does not match revenue growth, but the storage characteristics of baijiu base liquor make inventory turnover analysis of limited significance.

## IV. Risks, Contradictions, and Counter-Evidence

**Risk One (Observed risk facts): Contract liabilities decreased by 42.59% from the beginning of the year.** The original text attributes this to a decrease in advance payments from distributors [Current filing PDF p. 8]. Transmission path: decline in advance receipts → change in channel willingness to pay or settlement methods → if sustained, affects the pace of subsequent revenue recognition. Strongest alternative explanation: Advance receipts at the semi-annual point are themselves seasonal, and some sales shifted to bank acceptance bill settlement (notes receivable increased by 41.90%), so may simply be a substitution of settlement method rather than weakening demand. Verification condition: Whether the seesaw between contract liabilities and notes receivable continues in the third-quarter report.

**Risk Two (future risk still to be verified): The growth rate of selling expenses 24.56% far exceeded revenue growth 9.10%.** If market expense investment continues to increase while revenue growth does not recover, profit margins will come under pressure. Alternative explanation: Expense investment may be front-loaded, and the effect may be reflected with a lag. Expense details and investment structure for the current period are not obtained, and efficiency cannot determine.

**Risk Three (Insufficient evidence): Volume-price breakdown of revenue growth.** The original text for the current period only states "increased sales volume" [Current filing PDF p. 7], and the price contribution is not disclosed; The prior period explicitly mentioned price adjustments [Prior filing PDF p. 7]. In the absence of volume-price data, cannot determine whether the volume contribution to 9.10% growth is sustainable. Direction for supplementary evidence: disclosure of sales volume and per-tonne price in the annual report or operating data announcements.

## V. Scenarios and Follow-up Tracking

**Sensitive variables: channel advances from customers and expense deployment.** Base scenario (assumption): contract liabilities decline is seasonal, and selling expense growth converges with revenue growth, so full-year profit growth is roughly in line with revenue growth. Downside scenario (assumption): if advances from customers continue to decline and selling expenses maintain a growth rate of 20% or above, then profit growth may be lower than revenue growth, and revenue growth itself may decline further. The above are analytical assumptions, not company guidance.

**Tracking item one:** Contract liabilities, notes receivable, and selling expenses in the 2025 third-quarter report (subsequent actual disclosure pending verification). If contract liabilities period over period rebound and expense growth falls back, then the current-period signal is more likely seasonal; if they continue to deteriorate, the judgment on revenue sustainability needs to be lowered.

**Tracking item two:** Sales volume and per-tonne price data in the 2025 annual report (subsequent actual disclosure pending verification). If volume increases while price is flat or declines, then the shift in growth momentum is confirmed; if price still makes a positive contribution, then the current-period judgment needs to be revised.

**Tracking item three:** The scale of deposits absorbed and reserves in the notes related to the finance company. If the trend of net deposit decline continues, the gap between operating cash flow and net profit will persist, but this does not affect the cash-generating capacity of the core liquor business.
